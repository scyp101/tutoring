Before you book

Time zonesI work from Karachi (GMT+5). My calendar runs 10am to 10pm PKT, which covers European working hours and North American mornings.
Delivery windowThe 2, 7 and 14 day windows are Fiverr delivery timers rather than deadlines for your lessons. I extend them as we schedule.
How sessions runYou share your screen and do the modelling yourself while I guide you through it. A working install of SolidWorks or Fusion 360 is preferred.
Fiverr's feeFiverr adds its own service fee at checkout, so your total there will be a little higher than the prices shown here.
RecordingsIf you would like the session recorded, just let me know before we start and I will record it for you at no extra cost. You are welcome to record it yourself as well.
ReschedulingYou can reschedule yourself up to 6 hours before a session. Inside that window, please message me and I will move it wherever I can.
Exam readinessSOLIDWORKS recommends around 45 hours of hands-on time before CSWA and 100 before CSWP. Exam prep builds on that experience.
